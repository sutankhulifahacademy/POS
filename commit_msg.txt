### Backend (NEW: backend/routes/ai.py)
- POST /ai/assistant — Q&A with business data
  - Pattern matching for common questions
  - Returns: answer, facts, observations, recommendations, data_sources
  - Supports: top outlet, top products, total sales, late employees,
    most transactions, low stock, sales decline analysis, general overview
- GET /ai/daily-briefing — daily business summary
  - Today vs 7-day average comparison
  - Top outlet, low stock count, active shifts, attendance
- GET /ai/anomalies — anomaly detection
  - Sales spike/drop (>50% deviation)
  - Discount anomaly (>3x average)
  - Stockout detection (quantity <= 0)
  - Severity: critical, warning
- GET /ai/forecast — sales forecasting
  - Moving average based
  - Confidence: high/medium/low based on coefficient of variation
  - Returns "Insufficient data" if <7 days
